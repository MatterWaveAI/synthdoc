## Layout Variety Goals

### Complexity Types to Add:
1. **Multi-column layouts** (newspaper style, 3+ columns)
2. **Mixed orientation** (landscape tables in portrait doc)
3. **Dense grid forms** (like tax forms with many boxes)
4. **Overlay text** (watermarks, stamps, annotations)
5. **Handwriting simulation** (signature areas, filled fields)
6. **Table-heavy** (multi-page tables with headers)
7. **Hierarchical sections** (deeply nested numbering)
8. **Side-by-side forms** (duplicate forms on one page)
9. **Cut/tear-off sections** (perforated areas)
10. **Carbon copy style** (multiple copies indicated)
11. **Pre-printed form fills** (lined areas for handwriting)
12. **Check boxes and radio buttons** (form controls)
13. **Barcodes/QR codes** (machine-readable)
14. **Stamps and seals** (official markings)
15. **Marginalia** (side notes, annotations)
